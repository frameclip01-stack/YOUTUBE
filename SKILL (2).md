## 🏗️ The 5-Phase Narrative Architecture

Every Zack D. Films script is engineered through five strict, clockwork phases:

```mermaid
graph LR
    P1["Phase 1: Visceral Hook<br/>(0-5s / 1-18w)"] --> P2["Phase 2: Contextual Pivot<br/>(5-15s / 19-45w)"]
    P2 --> P3["Phase 3: Escalation/Mechanics<br/>(15-28s / 46-75w)"]
    P3 --> P4["Phase 4: Climax/Twist<br/>(28-36s / 76-95w)"]
    P4 --> P5["Phase 5: Closing Loop<br/>(36-42s / 96-115w)"]
    P5 -.->|"Seamless Loop"| P1
```

1. **Phase 1: In-Media-Res Visceral Hook (0-5s | 1-18 words)**
   - Drops the viewer directly into peak action, somatosensory shock, or an impossible riddle.
   - *Example:* *"He was pooping so hard that a blood vessel suddenly burst inside his brain..."*
2. **Phase 2: Contextual Pivot / Origin (5-15s | 19-45 words)**
   - Anchors the premise with *"You see..."*, *"Now, before..."*, or *"Imagine you..."*.
   - *Example:* *"Now, before the stroke, he had never cared about art."*
3. **Phase 3: Mechanical / Biological Escalation (15-28s | 46-75 words)**
   - Cause-and-effect chain explaining the hidden mechanics step-by-step with zero jargon.
   - *Example:* *"But doctors think the stroke damaged some parts of his brain while removing filters..."*
4. **Phase 4: Climax / Twist / Complication (28-36s | 76-95 words)**
   - The unexpected consequence, failure point, or shocking realization.
   - *Example:* *"This sucked glass back into the prosthetic eyeball, along with tissue from the eye socket..."*
5. **Phase 5: Punchline / Moral / Closing Loop (36-42s | 96-115 words)**
   - Ironic closure or thematic resolution engineered to feed smoothly back into the opening line.
   - *Example:* *"Meaning for one brief moment, our Earth was a sandwich."*

## 🎨 3D Visual Directing & AI Prompting Engine

The signature Zack D. Films visual aesthetic relies on:
- **Stylized Semi-Realistic 3D Humanoids**: Matte clay/silicone textures, expressive faces, avoiding uncanny valley.
- **Anatomical Cutaways & X-Ray Vision**: Translucent skin, internal organs, transparent gearboxes, cross-section slicing.
- **Lighting & Color**: Clean studio softbox, vibrant blue/purple rim light, solid pastel/dark gradient backgrounds.
- **Camera Dynamics**: Continuous rapid macro zooms, orbital pans, tracking shots.

### AI Prompt Template:
```text
3D animation, [Macro cross-section / X-ray cutaway / Character action] of [Subject] doing [Action], 
[Matte clay / Translucent fleshy / Metallic sheen] materials, subsurface scattering, medical visualization style, 
studio softbox lighting with vibrant rim light, [rapid forward zoom / slow orbital pan] camera motion, 4k resolution, clean background --ar 9:16
```
